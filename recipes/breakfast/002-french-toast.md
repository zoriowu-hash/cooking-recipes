# French Toast 法式吐司 (Sweet & Savory 甜鹹兩味)

Egg-soaked pan-fried bread, sweet or savory — 蛋液煎麵包，可甜可鹹 — Makes 5–6 slices · Prep 5 min · Cook 3 min per slice

## Ingredients 材料

**Base 基本**

- 5–6 slices thick bread (any kind; the video uses whole grain) 厚片麵包5–6片（任何麵包皆可，影片用全麥）
- 2 eggs, yolk and white beaten together 雞蛋兩隻（蛋黃蛋白一起打勻）
- 1 tsp vegetable oil + ½ tbsp butter, plus a little more between slices 植物油一茶匙 + 牛油半湯匙，每片之間再補少許

**Sweet version 甜味版** (add to the eggs 加入蛋液)

- ¼ cup milk 牛奶¼杯
- 1 tsp granulated sugar 砂糖一茶匙
- ¼ tsp cinnamon 肉桂粉¼茶匙
- Pinch of salt 鹽少許
- A few drops vanilla extract 雲呢拿香油幾滴
- Syrup, to serve 糖漿（享用時淋上）

**Savory version 鹹味版**

- Flax seeds 亞麻籽
- Herbs 香草
- Green onions, chopped 葱花
- Spices to sprinkle on top 撒面的香料

## Steps 做法

**Sweet version 甜味版**

1. Whisk the 2 eggs first, then add the milk, sugar, cinnamon, salt and vanilla. Mix well and pour into a plate with raised edges.
   先把兩隻雞蛋打勻，再加入牛奶、糖、肉桂粉、鹽和雲呢拿香油，拌勻後倒入有邊的碟子。
2. Heat a pan on medium heat (5/10) with the oil and butter for about a minute, until the butter starts to brown.
   平底鍋用中火（5/10）加熱，加油和牛油，等約一分鐘至牛油開始變色。
3. Quickly dip both sides of the bread in the egg mixture; let a little excess drip off.
   麵包兩面快速沾上蛋液，讓多餘的蛋液稍微滴走。
4. Fry about 1½ minutes per side, lifting a corner to check it isn't burning.
   每面煎約一分半鐘，可掀起一角檢查有沒有燒焦。
5. Add a little more butter and oil before each new slice. Serve with syrup.
   每煎一片前補少許牛油和油。淋上糖漿享用。

**Savory version 鹹味版**

1. Fry the flax seeds until fragrant.
   先把亞麻籽炒香。
2. Beat the eggs (yolk and white together), then mix in the flax seeds, herbs and green onions.
   雞蛋（蛋黃蛋白）打勻，加入亞麻籽、香草和葱花拌勻。
3. Dip the bread in the egg mixture and pan-fry the French toast way until golden on both sides (medium heat, ~1½ min per side).
   麵包沾上蛋液，按法式吐司的方法煎至兩面金黃（中火每面約一分半鐘）。
4. When it's almost done, sprinkle some spices on top.
   快煎好時，在麵包上撒些香料。

## Notes 備註

- **Thick bread works better.** 厚片麵包效果更好。
- **Frozen bread works too:** I tried bread straight from the freezer, still frozen, and it was good. It can't absorb much of the egg mixture, but that may actually be better.
  **冷凍麵包也可以：** 試過麵包從冰箱拿出來還是冷凍狀態就直接沾蛋液，效果不錯。它吸不了太多蛋液，但這樣可能反而更好。
- Source 來源: [Casual Cooking — How To Make French Toast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdgroNuyKdI) (sweet version 甜味版); savory version is my own 鹹味版是我自己的做法
- Variations 變化: Sweet or savory, as above. 甜鹹皆可，如上。
