# French Toast 法式吐司 (Sweet & Savory 甜咸两味)

Egg-soaked pan-fried bread, sweet or savory — 蛋液煎面包，可甜可咸 — Makes 5–6 slices · Prep 5 min · Cook 3 min per slice

## Ingredients 材料

**Base 基本**

- 5–6 slices thick bread (any kind; the video uses whole grain) 厚片面包5–6片（任何面包皆可，影片用全麦）
- 2 eggs, yolk and white beaten together 鸡蛋两只（蛋黄蛋白一起打匀）
- 1 tsp vegetable oil + ½ tbsp butter, plus a little more between slices 植物油一茶匙 + 牛油半汤匙，每片之间再补少许

**Sweet version 甜味版** (add to the eggs 加入蛋液)

- ¼ cup milk 牛奶¼杯
- 1 tsp granulated sugar 砂糖一茶匙
- ¼ tsp cinnamon 肉桂粉¼茶匙
- Pinch of salt 盐少许
- A few drops vanilla extract 云呢拿香油几滴
- Syrup, to serve 糖浆（享用时淋上）

**Savory version 咸味版**

- Flax seeds 亚麻籽
- Herbs 香草
- Green onions, chopped 葱花
- Spices to sprinkle on top 撒面的香料

## Steps 做法

**Sweet version 甜味版**

1. Whisk the 2 eggs first, then add the milk, sugar, cinnamon, salt and vanilla. Mix well and pour into a plate with raised edges.
   先把两只鸡蛋打匀，再加入牛奶、糖、肉桂粉、盐和云呢拿香油，拌匀后倒入有边的碟子。
2. Heat a pan on medium heat (5/10) with the oil and butter for about a minute, until the butter starts to brown.
   平底锅用中火（5/10）加热，加油和牛油，等约一分钟至牛油开始变色。
3. Quickly dip both sides of the bread in the egg mixture; let a little excess drip off.
   面包两面快速沾上蛋液，让多余的蛋液稍微滴走。
4. Fry about 1½ minutes per side, lifting a corner to check it isn't burning.
   每面煎约一分半钟，可掀起一角检查有没有烧焦。
5. Add a little more butter and oil before each new slice. Serve with syrup.
   每煎一片前补少许牛油和油。淋上糖浆享用。

**Savory version 咸味版**

1. Fry the flax seeds until fragrant.
   先把亚麻籽炒香。
2. Beat the eggs (yolk and white together), then mix in the flax seeds, herbs and green onions.
   鸡蛋（蛋黄蛋白）打匀，加入亚麻籽、香草和葱花拌匀。
3. Dip the bread in the egg mixture and pan-fry the French toast way until golden on both sides (medium heat, ~1½ min per side).
   面包沾上蛋液，按法式吐司的方法煎至两面金黄（中火每面约一分半钟）。
4. When it's almost done, sprinkle some spices on top.
   快煎好时，在面包上撒些香料。

## Notes 备注

- **Thick bread works better.** 厚片面包效果更好。
- **Frozen bread works too:** I tried bread straight from the freezer, still frozen, and it was good. It can't absorb much of the egg mixture, but that may actually be better.
  **冷冻面包也可以：** 试过面包从冰箱拿出来还是冷冻状态就直接沾蛋液，效果不错。它吸不了太多蛋液，但这样可能反而更好。
- Source 来源: [Casual Cooking — How To Make French Toast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdgroNuyKdI) (sweet version 甜味版); savory version is my own 咸味版是我自己的做法
- Variations 变化: Sweet or savory, as above. 甜咸皆可，如上。
