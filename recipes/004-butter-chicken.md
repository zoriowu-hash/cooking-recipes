# Butter Chicken 黄油鸡 (with Cashews 腰果版)

Yogurt-marinated chicken, grilled in butter, then simmered in a creamy tomato–cashew masala — 酸奶腌鸡肉，黄油煎香后放入奶香番茄腰果酱汁 — Serves 3–4 · Prep 15 min + marinating · Cook 45 min

> **Important 重点：** Don't overdo the cashews — too many make the dish heavy, so you can't eat much of it. More cashews is not better.
> **重要：腰果不要放太多——放多了菜会很腻，吃不了多少。腰果不是越多越好。**

## Ingredients 材料

**Marinade 腌料**

- ½ kg boneless chicken 去骨鸡肉500克
- 2 tbsp yogurt 酸奶两汤匙
- Pinch of saffron 藏红花少许
- 1 tsp paprika 红椒粉一茶匙
- 1 tsp coriander powder 芫荽粉一茶匙
- 1 tsp cumin powder 孜然粉一茶匙
- ½ tsp cinnamon powder 肉桂粉半茶匙
- 1 tsp cardamom powder 豆蔻粉一茶匙
- 1 tsp chili powder 辣椒粉一茶匙
- 2 tbsp garlic and ginger paste 姜蒜蓉两汤匙
- Juice of ½ lemon 半个柠檬的汁
- Salt to taste 盐适量
- Pinch of orange food colouring (optional) 橙色食用色素少许（可选）
- 50 g butter, to grill the chicken 黄油50克（煎鸡肉用）

**Masala 酱汁**

- 50 g butter 黄油50克
- 4 cardamom pods 豆蔻荚四颗
- 1 tbsp ginger and garlic paste 姜蒜蓉一汤匙
- 2 green chilies 青辣椒两只
- 1 medium onion 中等洋葱一个
- 2 large tomatoes 大番茄两个
- 15–20 cashew nuts — no more (see Notes) 腰果15–20颗——不要多放（见备注）
- 1 tsp garam masala 印度综合香料一茶匙
- 1 tsp red chili powder 红辣椒粉一茶匙
- 1 tsp paprika powder 红椒粉一茶匙
- 1 tsp turmeric powder 姜黄粉一茶匙
- 1 tsp nutmeg powder 肉豆蔻粉一茶匙
- 1 tsp sugar 糖一茶匙
- Salt to taste 盐适量
- 1 cup water 水一杯
- 50 ml double cream 浓奶油50毫升
- 1 tbsp butter, to finish 黄油一汤匙（最后加）

## Steps 做法

The video description lists ingredients but not step-by-step instructions; these steps follow its outline (marinate → grill → masala). 影片说明只列出材料，以下步骤按其流程（腌制 → 煎 → 酱汁）整理。

**Chicken 鸡肉**

1. Cut the chicken into pieces and mix with all the marinade ingredients (except the butter). Marinate for at least 30 minutes, longer if you can.
   鸡肉切块，和所有腌料（黄油除外）拌匀，腌至少30分钟，能久一点更好。
2. Melt the 50 g butter in a pan and grill/sear the chicken until browned and cooked through. Set aside.
   锅中放50克黄油融化，把鸡肉煎至上色熟透，盛起备用。

**Masala 酱汁**

3. Melt the 50 g butter, add the cardamom pods, then the ginger-garlic paste and green chilies; sauté until fragrant.
   融化50克黄油，放入豆蔻荚，再加姜蒜蓉和青辣椒炒香。
4. Add the chopped onion and cook until soft, then the chopped tomatoes and the cashews. Cook until the tomatoes break down.
   加入切碎的洋葱炒软，再加番茄碎和腰果，煮到番茄软烂。
5. Add salt, garam masala, red chili powder, paprika, turmeric, nutmeg and sugar, plus the water. Simmer, then blend into a smooth sauce.
   加入盐、印度综合香料、红辣椒粉、红椒粉、姜黄粉、肉豆蔻粉和糖，再加水煮一会儿，然后打成顺滑的酱汁。
6. Return the sauce to the pan, add the chicken and simmer until the sauce thickens.
   酱汁倒回锅中，放入鸡肉，煮到酱汁变稠。
7. Stir in the cream and finish with 1 tbsp butter. Serve with naan.
   拌入浓奶油，最后加一汤匙黄油。配印度烤饼（馕）享用。

## Notes 备注

- **Important 重点：** I added too many cashews once — it made the dish heavy, so you can't eat much of it. More cashews is not better; stick to 15–20 or fewer.
  **重要：我有一次腰果放太多了——菜变得很腻，吃不了多少。腰果不是越多越好，15–20颗或更少就够了。**
- Source 来源: [Imran.Family Kitchen — Authentic Butter Chicken Recipe with Cashews](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mq801G1OzMU)
- Variations 变化: The orange food colouring is only for looks and can be skipped. 橙色色素只是为了颜色，可以不放。
