# Doenjang Soup 大醬湯 (Doenjang-jjigae)

Korean soybean paste stew with pork, vegetables and tofu on an anchovy–kombu broth — 韓式大醬湯 — Serves 2–3 · Prep 20 min · Cook 40 min

## Ingredients 材料

**Broth 湯底** (can substitute store-bought chicken stock + water 可用現成雞湯加水代替)

- 1 L water 水一公升 (rice-rinsing water 淘米水 — see Notes)
- 1 piece kombu 昆布一塊
- 5 dried anchovies, heads and guts removed 鯷魚乾五條（去頭及內臟）
- 2 cloves garlic 蒜頭兩粒

**Soup 湯料**

- ½ onion 洋葱半個
- 1 potato 馬鈴薯一個
- ½ zucchini 櫛瓜半條
- Assorted mushrooms, to taste 各種菇類適量
- 1 leek / Korean green onion 京葱一條
- 1 chili 辣椒一條
- 1 block tofu 豆腐一塊
- 150 g pork, half-fat half-lean 半肥瘦豬肉150克
- 700 ml broth (from above) 上湯700毫升
- ½ tbsp oil 油半湯匙

**Seasoning 調味料**

- 2 tbsp doenjang (Korean soybean paste) 大醬兩湯匙
- 1 tbsp minced garlic 蒜蓉一湯匙
- 1 tbsp soju 韓國燒酒一湯匙
- 1 tsp sugar 糖一茶匙

## Steps 做法

**Broth 湯底**

1. Remove the heads and guts from the anchovies.
   鯷魚去頭部和內臟備用。
2. In a pot, add 1 L water (ideally rice-rinsing water), the anchovies, kombu and garlic. Cover and simmer on low heat for 20 minutes, then strain out the solids and keep the broth.
   在鍋子裏加入水（最好用淘米水）一公升、鯷魚、昆布和蒜頭，加蓋以小火煮二十分鐘，之後盛起湯料，留下上湯備用。

**Soup 大醬湯**

3. Cut the onion, potato, zucchini, mushrooms, tofu and pork into small pieces. Dice the leek and chili.
   洋葱、馬鈴薯、櫛瓜、各種菇類、豆腐、半肥瘦豬肉切成小塊；京葱、辣椒切粒。
4. Heat ½ tbsp oil in a soup pot and stir-fry the pork until fragrant.
   在湯鍋裏加油半湯匙，炒香豬肉。
5. Add the onion, leek, doenjang, minced garlic, soju, chili, sugar and potato, then pour in the anchovy broth.
   放入洋葱、京葱、大醬、蒜蓉、燒酒、辣椒、糖和馬鈴薯，加入之前做好的鯷魚上湯。
6. Add the zucchini and mushrooms, then the tofu last. Simmer for 20 minutes and it's done.
   加入櫛瓜和菇類，最後加入豆腐，煮20分鐘便完成。

## Notes 備註

- **Important 重點：** The key is the broth — simmer the stock with rice-rinsing water, dried anchovies and kombu.
  **重要的事：淘米水，鱼干（anchovy）和昆布吊高汤。**
- Source 來源: [EAT AT HOME 食・家 — 大醬三味 Three Ways to Enjoy Doenjang](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3yIQNUf4KCg&t=245s) (recipe 2 of 3 in the video; the other two are boiled pork belly 韓式水煮肉 and seasoned cucumber chili 涼拌青瓜辣椒)
- Variations 變化: Short on time? Use store-bought chicken stock diluted with water instead of the anchovy broth. 趕時間可用現成雞湯加水代替鯷魚上湯。
