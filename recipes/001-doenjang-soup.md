# Doenjang Soup 大酱汤 (Doenjang-jjigae)

Korean soybean paste stew with pork, vegetables and tofu on an anchovy–kombu broth — 韩式大酱汤 — Serves 2–3 · Prep 20 min · Cook 40 min

## Ingredients 材料

**Broth 汤底** (can substitute store-bought chicken stock + water 可用现成鸡汤加水代替)

- 1 L water 水一公升 (rice-rinsing water 淘米水 — see Notes)
- 1 piece kombu 昆布一块
- 5 dried anchovies, heads and guts removed 鳀鱼干五条（去头及内脏）
- 2 cloves garlic 蒜头两粒

**Soup 汤料**

- ½ onion 洋葱半个
- 1 potato 马铃薯一个
- ½ zucchini 栉瓜半条
- Assorted mushrooms, to taste 各种菇类适量
- 1 leek / Korean green onion 京葱一条
- 1 chili 辣椒一条
- 1 block tofu 豆腐一块
- 150 g pork, half-fat half-lean 半肥瘦猪肉150克
- 700 ml broth (from above) 上汤700毫升
- ½ tbsp oil 油半汤匙

**Seasoning 调味料**

- 2 tbsp doenjang (Korean soybean paste) 大酱两汤匙
- 1 tbsp minced garlic 蒜蓉一汤匙
- 1 tbsp soju 韩国烧酒一汤匙
- 1 tsp sugar 糖一茶匙

## Steps 做法

**Broth 汤底**

1. Remove the heads and guts from the anchovies.
   鳀鱼去头部和内脏备用。
2. In a pot, add 1 L water (ideally rice-rinsing water), the anchovies, kombu and garlic. Cover and simmer on low heat for 20 minutes, then strain out the solids and keep the broth.
   在锅子里加入水（最好用淘米水）一公升、鳀鱼、昆布和蒜头，加盖以小火煮二十分钟，之后盛起汤料，留下上汤备用。

**Soup 大酱汤**

3. Cut the onion, potato, zucchini, mushrooms, tofu and pork into small pieces. Dice the leek and chili.
   洋葱、马铃薯、栉瓜、各种菇类、豆腐、半肥瘦猪肉切成小块；京葱、辣椒切粒。
4. Heat ½ tbsp oil in a soup pot and stir-fry the pork until fragrant.
   在汤锅里加油半汤匙，炒香猪肉。
5. Add the onion, leek, doenjang, minced garlic, soju, chili, sugar and potato, then pour in the anchovy broth.
   放入洋葱、京葱、大酱、蒜蓉、烧酒、辣椒、糖和马铃薯，加入之前做好的鳀鱼上汤。
6. Add the zucchini and mushrooms, then the tofu last. Simmer for 20 minutes and it's done.
   加入栉瓜和菇类，最后加入豆腐，煮20分钟便完成。

## Notes 备注

- **Important 重点：** The key is the broth — simmer the stock with rice-rinsing water, dried anchovies and kombu.
  **重要的事：淘米水，鱼干（anchovy）和昆布吊高汤。**
- Source 来源: [EAT AT HOME 食・家 — 大酱三味 Three Ways to Enjoy Doenjang](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3yIQNUf4KCg&t=245s) (recipe 2 of 3 in the video; the other two are boiled pork belly 韩式水煮肉 and seasoned cucumber chili 凉拌青瓜辣椒)
- Variations 变化: Short on time? Use store-bought chicken stock diluted with water instead of the anchovy broth. 赶时间可用现成鸡汤加水代替鳀鱼上汤。
