# Pad Kra Pao 泰式打抛牛肉 (Thai Holy Basil Beef Stir-Fry)

The "original", old-school version of Thailand's favourite stir-fry: seared beef, chili-garlic, fish sauce and basil, topped with a crispy fried egg — 老派做法的泰式打抛，配脆边煎蛋 — Serves 2 · Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min

> **Important 重点：** Use **holy basil** — or Italian basil if you can't find it. **Fish sauce is very important.**
> **重要：用圣罗勒（打抛叶）——买不到就用意大利罗勒。鱼露非常重要。**

## Ingredients 材料

Quantities aren't given in the video; adjust to taste. 影片没有给出份量，按口味调整。

**Stir-fry 炒料**

- Ground or minced beef 牛肉碎
- Thai chilies (spicy), as many as you can handle 泰国小辣椒（辣），按能接受的量
- Milder chilies 较温和的辣椒
- Lots of garlic 大量蒜头
- Fish sauce 鱼露
- A little sugar 少许糖
- Splash of water, or unsalted chicken/beef stock 少许水，或无盐鸡汤/牛肉汤
- Mild peppers or bell pepper, julienned 温和辣椒或甜椒，切丝
- Holy basil (or Italian basil; Thai basil also works) 圣罗勒（或意大利罗勒；泰国罗勒也可以）
- Oil 油

**Thai-style fried egg 泰式煎蛋**

- 1 egg per person 每人一只鸡蛋
- Plenty of oil 较多的油

**Prik nam pla 鱼露辣椒汁** (optional, for the egg 可选，淋在蛋上)

- Chopped chilies 辣椒碎
- Fish sauce 鱼露
- Squeeze of lime 青柠汁少许

## Steps 做法

1. Pound the Thai chilies into a fine paste in a mortar (no big chunks), then add the garlic and milder chilies and pound into a rough paste.
   用石臼把泰国小辣椒捣成细蓉（不要有大块），再加入蒜头和温和辣椒，捣成粗蓉。
2. Get the wok very hot, until it starts to smoke. Add the beef and spread it thin so it sizzles and browns.
   把锅烧到非常热、开始冒烟，放入牛肉摊薄，让它滋滋作响并煎出焦色。
3. Flip, toss, re-spread and let it sear again. Halfway through the beef releases liquid and stops browning — that's normal. Keep cooking until the water evaporates and the sound changes from simmering to sizzling, then let it sear again without stirring too much.
   翻面、翻炒、再摊开继续煎。中途牛肉会出水，不再上色——这是正常的。继续煮到水分蒸发、声音从"咕嘟"变成"滋滋"，然后少翻动，让它再次煎出焦色。
4. Turn the heat down, push the beef to one side, add a little oil and sauté the chili-garlic paste.
   转小火，把牛肉推到一边，加少许油，炒香辣椒蒜蓉。
5. Turn the heat back up and mix the beef back in. Add a splash of water (or unsalted stock) to make some sauce.
   转回大火，把牛肉和蒜蓉拌匀。加少许水（或无盐高汤）做出一点酱汁。
6. Season with fish sauce and a little sugar — that's all. Cook until the sauce is the consistency you like.
   只用鱼露和少许糖调味。煮到酱汁浓稠度合适。
7. Add the julienned peppers, turn off the heat, and stir in all the basil — residual heat is enough to wilt it.
   加入辣椒丝，关火，放入全部罗勒拌匀——余温就足够让它变软。
8. Fry the egg in plenty of hot oil until the whites are crispy and bubbly, keeping the yolk runny.
   用较多的热油煎蛋，煎到蛋白边缘酥脆起泡，蛋黄保持流心。
9. Serve over rice with the egg on top, and spoon a little prik nam pla over the egg.
   盛在米饭上，放上煎蛋，在蛋上淋少许鱼露辣椒汁。

## Notes 备注

- **Important 重点：** Use holy basil — either holy basil or Italian basil. Fish sauce is very important.
  **重要：用圣罗勒——圣罗勒或意大利罗勒都可以。鱼露非常重要。**
- Basil 罗勒: Holy basil is hard to find outside Thailand. Italian basil tastes closer to it than Thai basil does, but Thai basil is also delicious.
  圣罗勒在泰国以外很难买到。意大利罗勒的味道比泰国罗勒更接近圣罗勒，不过用泰国罗勒也很好吃。
- Sear the beef well: browning gives it a robust, grilled flavour and a chewier texture. 牛肉一定要煎出焦色：这样才有浓郁的烤香，口感也更有嚼劲。
- Don't skip the fried egg: the runny yolk binds the dry stir-fry together. 不要省略煎蛋：流心蛋黄能让偏干的炒料更融合。
- Source 来源: [Pailin's Kitchen — The "Original" Thai Basil Stir Fry (Pad Kra Pao)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xG_jL8luhF0) · written recipe with quantities 有份量的文字食谱: https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/pad-kra-pao-beef/
- Variations 变化: Use two kinds of chilies (spicy + mild) so there's lots of chili flavour without too much heat. 用两种辣椒（辣 + 温和），辣椒味足但不会太辣。
